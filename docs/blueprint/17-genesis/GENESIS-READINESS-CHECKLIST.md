## Gate rules

`FOUNDATION EXECUTION AUTHORIZED` requires all Stage 1 prerequisites to pass.

`IMPLEMENTATION CERTIFIED` requires Stage 2 completion plus all required Stage 3 evidence.

`PRODUCTION CERTIFIED` requires Stage 4 operational evidence.

A design change after freeze requires a new ADR and an updated freeze record.

Evidence work that conforms to the frozen design does not reopen architectural decisions.
