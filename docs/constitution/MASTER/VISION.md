# A-L-L-A-H Ecosystem — Master Vision

**Status:** DECIDED for product separation; PROPOSED for broader ecosystem framing  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-15

## North star

Build a coherent ecosystem in which personal creation and public witnessing remain distinct, while approved ideas, stories, and works can move between them deliberately.

The ecosystem has two independently owned products:

- **ROCKSOUL DX** is a private, desktop-first application for its single primary user: the product owner.
- **MoonWitness** is a public-facing web product for stories, testimony, research, messages, and selected publications.

They share a wider creative and narrative context, not a shared user experience or unrestricted data pool.

## The two-product model

### ROCKSOUL DX — private desktop creation

ROCKSOUL DX is a **desktop application**: the owner's personal environment for thinking, exploration, memory, creative work, research, and development of the ROCKSOUL character universe.

Its primary interaction model is designed for desktop use: a persistent personal workspace, direct manipulation of creative material, local file/artifact workflows, and deliberate transitions between research, memory, and creation. It must not be specified as a browser-first website or a MoonWitness-style web dashboard.

Its internal workspace may contain unfinished ideas, personal records, drafts, reflections, private source material, and experiments. These remain private unless explicitly selected for export or publication.

**Scope decision:** the owner has approved a layered ROCKSOUL desktop universe combining continuity, creative/research spaces, and an execution space. The owner has also approved the combined GAME WORLD + LIVING ANIMATION experience direction. The v0.1 execution boundary is a visual foundation prototype: THE THRESHOLD plus navigable shells for THE ARCHIVE, THE STUDIO, THE RIOT DECK, and THE SIGNAL ROOM, with THE RIOT DECK receiving the first detailed room shell. Full workflows remain separately gated.

The owner has selected Tauri as the desktop framework direction. Frontend framework, runtime boundaries, operating-system support, packaging/signing, and local/hybrid storage remain open technical decisions to be documented through contracts and ADRs.

Product promise:

> A private desktop universe to preserve continuity, explore ideas, create work, research, and execute bounded projects — through distinct spaces unified by a human-controlled continuity core.

Experience identity: **THE LIVING UNIVERSE — GAME WORLD + LIVING ANIMATION**. Visual core: **NOISE × SILENCE**. Entry: **THE THRESHOLD**. Normative details are in the ROCKSOUL DX Visual Constitution.

### MoonWitness — public witnessing

MoonWitness is a web product for presenting selected stories, messages, research, archives, and creative work to an audience.

Its public identity is grounded in the established line:

> When the Moon Witnesses, We Share A Message.

MoonWitness must distinguish a documented event from a dream, observation, research finding, interpretation, coincidence, joke, fictional narrative, or unknown. Publication must not erase those distinctions.

## ROCKSOUL operating philosophy

The current owner-supplied phrase is **KEEP THE THREAD — JUST DO!T — LEVEL UP!!**. The associated personal motto is **NATURAL BIAR HIDUPMU MENJADI SUPRANATURAL**. Preserve their wording; any explanation of their meaning remains a proposed editorial interpretation until the owner approves it. These phrases do not authorize unsafe actions or imply that software/AI can know divine intent.

The proposed continuity principle is to help the owner preserve context, revisit unfinished threads, understand sources and uncertainty, create, review, and return. It is not a compulsory linear workflow or a productivity score.

## Shared foundations, separate products

The products may refer to shared canon such as GOD PROMISE / JANJI ALLAH, RGBL / SRGBL, ROCKSOUL characters, MYLUFT, Human Pen, BASS-HUNTER, and selected MoonWitness story material.

This does not make every concept a software module. Some are spiritual foundations, some are narrative canon, some are knowledge frameworks, and some are creative identities. Their category and source must be recorded before implementation is proposed.

## Non-goals

- Do not turn ROCKSOUL DX into a public social network.
- Do not turn ROCKSOUL DX into a browser-first web dashboard or MoonWitness admin panel.
- Do not turn MoonWitness into the owner's private desktop workspace.
- Do not automatically publish or synchronize private ROCKSOUL data.
- Do not collapse all characters, collectives, and projects into interchangeable avatars or features.
- Do not invent definitions for unresolved canon, including the exact expansions and formal structure of RGBL / SRGBL.
- Do not represent spiritual interpretation, numerology, dreams, or personal meaning as independently verified fact.
- Do not treat a proposed architecture or roadmap item as an implemented capability.

## Success criteria

1. Each product has a clear purpose, audience, and repository boundary.
2. The owner can create and retain private material in ROCKSOUL DX without accidental publication.
3. ROCKSOUL DX's desktop interaction model is explicit and not inherited from MoonWitness or Master Hub.
4. MoonWitness can publish selected material with context, provenance, and editorial control.
5. Shared terms and canon have traceable sources and explicit confidence/status.
6. Integrations are narrow, versioned, permissioned, and reversible.
7. The products can evolve independently without requiring a monolithic codebase.

## Product-owner authority

The product owner is the final authority for product direction and personal/narrative canon. Engineering recommendations may clarify consequences and trade-offs but must not rewrite the owner's story or spiritual commitments.
