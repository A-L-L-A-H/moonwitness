# Ecosystem Core Principles

**Status:** DECIDED unless marked as product-specific  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-15

## 1. Human authorship

AI may assist with recall, analysis, exploration, drafting, and creation. It must not silently claim authorship of the owner's identity, memories, beliefs, or decisions.

## 2. Private by default

Personal notes, drafts, conversations, memories, source material, and unfinished work in ROCKSOUL DX are private by default. Publication requires an explicit user action and a clear preview of what will leave the private environment.

## 3. Witness before conclusion

Preserve the difference between what happened, what was observed, what was reported, what was researched, and what someone thinks it means.

## 4. Evidence and interpretation remain distinguishable

A source, observation, inference, hypothesis, belief, metaphor, and fictional device are different kinds of information. The system must not flatten them into one undifferentiated truth claim.

## 5. Canon is not generated casually

Character history, relationships, names, spiritual references, and established story details must be sourced. New creative proposals are allowed, but they must be labeled as proposals until approved.

## 6. Preserve the journey

Corrections and changes should not silently erase prior decisions or historical context. Keep meaningful provenance and superseded records where appropriate.

## 7. Creation over performance

The products should enable meaningful work without forcing users into productivity theatre, public metrics, streaks, or engagement loops.

## 8. Distinct identities

ROCKSOUL DX and MoonWitness have different users, product promises, and interfaces. Shared foundations must not make their experiences generic or interchangeable.

## 9. Spiritual adab and epistemic humility

GOD PROMISE / JANJI ALLAH is treated with respect. The software must not claim divine authority, speak on behalf of Allah, manufacture theological certainty, or present a personal interpretation as a religious ruling. References to scripture must retain accurate context and source attribution.

## 10. Accessibility and user control

Motion, sound, visual effects, and AI assistance must be controllable. Provide reduced-motion behavior and clear controls for data export, deletion, and publication as appropriate to each product.

## 11. Security is a product feature

Use least privilege, explicit authorization, secure defaults, auditable publication, and separation of public and private data.

## 12. Build only what has a defined owner and purpose

Every capability needs a user, a reason to exist, a product boundary, and a testable outcome. Avoid premature service splitting and speculative infrastructure.
