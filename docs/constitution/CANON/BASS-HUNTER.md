## Safe interpretation boundary

The framework may be used as a way to organize personal questions, experiences, observations, research, and reflection. It must preserve the difference between:

- a blessing or personal expression of gratitude;
- an answer someone believes they have received;
- a sign or perceived correspondence;
- an observation that can be independently checked;
- a scientific claim supported by a suitable method and evidence.

These categories must not be treated as interchangeable. A perceived sign or meaningful coincidence is not, by itself, proof of causation, a scientific finding, or evidence of divine intent.
