# ROCKSOUL Research Steward Topology

## Status: BOOTSTRAP MODE

ROCKSOUL is still building its first meaningful research corpus. The system therefore uses the maximum **five scheduled intelligence slots**, all running **hourly** and staggered by ten minutes.

> **SHARE THE CLOCK. NEVER SHARE THE CANONICAL MIND.**

```text
:00  SLOT 1  ECOSYSTEM BOOTSTRAP   → CRAYON
:10  SLOT 2  STORY-HISTORY         → MFTL → context reset → LEGEND
:20  SLOT 3  ATTESTATION           → RGBL → context reset → SUPERHERO
:30  SLOT 4  LAW                   → AWS
:40  SLOT 5  PERSPECTIVE           → JIZZ

RELATIONSHIP / CORRELATION
→ event-driven review
→ no scheduled intelligence slot
```

## Why all five run hourly

The current bottleneck is not steady-state maintenance. It is **corpus formation**. The hourly bootstrap phase is designed to increase:

- qualified discovery leads;
- reviewable candidate queues;
- domain coverage;
- geographic/source diversity;
- freshness awareness;
- cross-domain routing opportunities.

Hourly does **not** mean forced commits. Every Steward may return `NO_UPDATE` when no meaningful action exists.

## Slot 1 — Ecosystem Bootstrap

CRAYON owns orchestration, not research canon.

It may inspect all current `main` branches, CI, queues, source freshness and cross-domain leads. It may route research work to domain owners. It must not mint STORY, EVENT, PERSON, TEXT, LAW, PERSPECTIVE or RELATIONSHIP records.

Global discovery dedup and domain dedup are separate:

```text
same source already discovered
≠
same domain research object
```

A single source may legitimately support different bounded views in multiple owner repositories.

## Slot 2 — Story-History

One clock, two isolated passes:

```text
MFTL
STORY only
finish
↓
DESTROY / RESET CONTEXT
↓
LEGEND
EVENT only
```

Narrative evidence does not become historical-event truth merely because the same Steward cycle encountered it.

## Slot 3 — Attestation

One clock, two isolated passes:

```text
RGBL
TEXT only
finish
↓
DESTROY / RESET CONTEXT
↓
SUPERHERO
PERSON only
```

A textual referent does not automatically become a canonical PERSON identity.

## Slot 4 — LAW

AWS remains dedicated even during bootstrap because the cost of incorrect applicability, jurisdiction or authority reasoning is high.

Deterministic source monitors should do polling/fingerprinting where possible; the Steward should spend intelligence on review, interpretation, uncertainty and legal research gaps.

## Slot 5 — PERSPECTIVE

JIZZ remains dedicated and receives the highest discovery allowance because PERSPECTIVE is the highest-velocity research domain.

Hourly runs prioritize:

- distinct actor classes;
- multiple geographies/languages;
- framing diversity;
- reaction diversity;
- emerging/declining signals;
- perspective changes over time.

Article volume is not consensus.

## Correlation is event-driven

Correlation does not spend one of the five scheduled intelligence slots.

```text
domain research
    ↓
reviewable relation proposal
    ↓
Correlation queue
    ↓
support + counterevidence + alternatives + provenance
    ↓
RELATIONSHIP
```

This prevents scheduled pattern hunting.

## Bootstrap exit

Do not reduce cadence simply because a raw item count was reached. Reassess bootstrap mode only when all research owners have:

- a healthy non-duplicate queue;
- a meaningful canonical baseline;
- usable source diversity;
- usable geographic/tradition coverage where relevant;
- stable validation;
- lower marginal value from another hourly discovery cycle.

Until then, hourly is intentional.


## Scheduled intelligence vs deterministic provider jobs

The five-slot limit applies to **scheduled intelligence Stewards**, not every deterministic source monitor or Scout.

The invariant is:

```text
PROVIDER MAY DIFFER
CADENCE MAY DIFFER
          ↓
DISCOVERY
          ↓
OWNER-REPO ISSUE CONTRACT v1
          ↓
SAME LIFECYCLE
```

Examples:

- JIZZ may keep a GDELT broad-discovery fallback because PERSPECTIVE is high velocity.
- MFTL may use OpenAlex/Crossref scholarly discovery.
- AWS may run official-source/freshness monitors.

Those secondary jobs do not create extra canonical minds and do not bypass the five hourly Steward slots. They must deduplicate and open/update an owner-repository research issue before candidate or canonical work.
