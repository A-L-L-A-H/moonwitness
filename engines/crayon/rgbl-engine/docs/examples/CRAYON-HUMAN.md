# CRAYON–HUMAN — SELF

Core question: **Who am I, what shapes me, and what do my choices mean?**

## R1 — Information
Observe body state, words, actions, emotions, needs, memory, behavior, context, and change.

## G2 — Knowledge
Identify habits, triggers, responses, recurring emotional patterns, learning loops, and cause-effect hypotheses.

## B3 — Understanding / Meaning
Examine agency, intention, relationship, impact, responsibility, role, values, and purpose.

## SIN-CAN
A mistake is data about a condition; it is not automatically the whole identity of a person.

```text
MISTAKE → LOOK → UNDERSTAND → POSSIBILITY → RESPONSIBLE CHOICE → LEARN
```

## L4 — Wisdom
Self-awareness should produce humility, empathy, discipline, better choice, repair, growth, and responsible living—not merely self-description.
