# CRAYON–NATURE — WORLD

Core question: **How does observable reality work?**

## R1 — Information
Observe and measure natural phenomena. Record conditions, units, uncertainty, and missing variables.

## G2 — Knowledge
Find patterns, mechanisms, cycles, relationships, models, and testable predictions.

## B3 — Understanding / Meaning
Identify living systems, dependencies, ecological relationships, human participation, vulnerability, and impact.

## L4 — Wisdom
Use knowledge with humility, stewardship, sustainability, caution, and responsibility.

Scientific models remain open to revision when better evidence appears.
