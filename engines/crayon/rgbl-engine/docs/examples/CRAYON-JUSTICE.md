# CRAYON–JUSTICE — OTHERS

Core question: **What is fair, who is affected, and what decision is responsible?**

## R1 — Information
Establish what happened, who was present, what changed, what evidence exists, and what remains unknown.

## G2 — Knowledge
Identify applicable rules, recurring conflict patterns, ownership/turn-taking structures, cause-effect chains, and precedents where relevant.

## B3 — Understanding / Meaning
Examine victim/affected parties, actors, witnesses, intention, rights, relationship, impact, responsibility, and possibilities for repair.

## L4 — Wisdom
Seek fairness, proportionality, empathy, accountability, caution, restoration, and a decision that can be explained and reviewed.

> Facts first. Reason carefully. Decide fairly.
