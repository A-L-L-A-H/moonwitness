# CRAYON–ALLAH — SOURCE

## Purpose

CRAYON–ALLAH is a domain application for organizing theological inquiry. It is not a claim that Allah can be empirically measured by the framework.

## Flow

### R1 — Information
Observe creation, change, dependence, life, order, human questioning, and source materials. Keep observation distinct from theological interpretation.

### G2 — Knowledge
Examine regularity, dependence, explanatory models, philosophical arguments, and the relationship among claims.

### B3 — Understanding / Meaning
Ask what the inquiry means for the human being who seeks, reasons, chooses, worships, doubts, returns, and takes responsibility.

### Revelation source
For Islamic theological claims about Allah, revelation is a domain source. Attributes stated from revelation should be labeled as revelation-based theological claims, not empirical measurements.

### L4 — Wisdom / Recognition
Integrate the inquiry into responsible theological recognition, humility, responsibility, worship/orientation, caution, and further study.

## Boundary

```text
RECOGNITION ≠ EMPIRICAL MEASUREMENT OF ALLAH
```

The framework organizes signs, reasoning, source types, interpretations, and limits.
