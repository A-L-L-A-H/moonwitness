# 13 Way

The 13 Way is the inquiry-to-life pathway:

1. Observe
2. Notice
3. Question
4. Measure
5. Compare
6. Explore
7. Understand
8. Connect
9. Evaluate
10. Reason
11. Conclude
12. Act
13. Live

It is not necessarily a rigid one-pass checklist. Real inquiry may loop backward when new evidence or contradiction appears.

A conclusion that jumps from **Observe** directly to **Conclude** without measurement, comparison, alternatives, or reasoning should be treated as weak until checked.
