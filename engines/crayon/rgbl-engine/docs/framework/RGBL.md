# RGBL

RGBL is the four-level cumulative reasoning structure at the center of CRAYON.

## R1 — Reality

Question: **What is actually present or observable?**

Required yield: **Information**.

Typical contents:
- facts;
- observations;
- measurements;
- source records;
- time and context;
- explicit unknowns.

R1 must distinguish observation from interpretation.

## G2 — Governing Order

Question: **What supported pattern, relation, rule, mechanism, or model can be identified?**

Required yield: **Knowledge**.

Typical contents:
- patterns;
- correlations;
- causal hypotheses;
- rules;
- mechanisms;
- comparisons;
- models.

G2 depends on R1. Pattern claims should not be stronger than the information supporting them.

## B3 — Being

Question: **Who or what exists, acts, experiences, depends, intends, relates, and is affected?**

Required yield: **Understanding / Meaning**.

Typical contents:
- being and identity in context;
- agency;
- intention;
- relationship;
- impact;
- value;
- responsibility;
- meaning.

B3 is not merely biography. It asks what the pattern means for beings inside the system.

## L4 — Light

Question: **What integrated wisdom or responsible direction follows?**

Required yield: **Wisdom / Responsible Direction**.

Typical contents:
- integrated lesson;
- decision direction;
- benefit;
- caution;
- confidence and limitation;
- responsible action;
- next question.

L4 is not permission to make unsupported claims. Wisdom must remain accountable to R1, G2, and B3.
