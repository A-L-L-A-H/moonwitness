# 9 Gate

The 9 Gate layer describes access to information before higher-order synthesis.

1. **See**
2. **Hear**
3. **Touch**
4. **Taste**
5. **Smell**
6. **Attend**
7. **Remember**
8. **Reason**
9. **Choose**

The first five emphasize sensory access; the last four emphasize attentional, memory, reasoning, and choice functions.

## RGBL Perception

The incoming material is transformed through:

```text
R1 Receive
G2 Differentiate
B3 Integrate
L4 Orient
```

A Gate is not the same as a conclusion. Receiving information does not guarantee that the interpretation is correct.
