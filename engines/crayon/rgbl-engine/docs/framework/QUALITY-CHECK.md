# Quality Check

Before a CRAYON conclusion is treated as usable, check four areas.

## Evidence

- What supports the claim?
- What is directly observed?
- What comes from a source?
- What remains unknown?

## Alternatives

- What other explanations are plausible?
- What would disconfirm the preferred explanation?

## Reasoning

- Does the conclusion follow from the evidence?
- Was correlation confused with causation?
- Were assumptions made visible?

## Confidence

Confidence is not a decoration. It should reflect evidentiary coverage, uncertainty, alternatives, and limitations.

The engine exposes confidence as a workflow indicator, not a universal probability-of-truth calculator.

## Rule

> **Jangan asal yakin.**

A confident tone is not a substitute for evidence.
