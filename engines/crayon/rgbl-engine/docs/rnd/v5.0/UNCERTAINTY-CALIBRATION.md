# RGBL v5.0 — Uncertainty, Calibration & Decision Thresholds

Confidence is decomposed into distinct uncertainties: epistemic, variability, source, model, and normative.

## Calibration
For predictions with observable binary outcomes, tooling can compute Brier score and calibration bins/ECE-style summaries. These metrics apply only where a forecast/outcome interpretation is valid.

## Decisions
There is no universal confidence cutoff. Required support rises with risk and irreversibility. Below threshold, the preferred output may be a reversible test, information gathering, or stopping rather than forced action.
