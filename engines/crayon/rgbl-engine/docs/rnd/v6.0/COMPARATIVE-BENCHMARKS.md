# RGBL v6.0 — Comparative, Holdout & Adversarial Evaluation

## Comparison arms
A study may compare free reasoning, a neutral checklist baseline, and RGBL-assisted reasoning.

## Candidate outcome dimensions
- unsupported claim rate;
- provenance coverage;
- UNKNOWN honesty;
- transition quality;
- decision proportionality;
- confidence calibration where outcomes exist.

## Holdout discipline
Holdout case IDs/content are frozen before final analysis. Exact content-hash overlap with development/validation is a release-stopping leakage error for the benchmark.

## Adversarial families
Emotional framing, authority bias, missing context, contradictory evidence, causal bait, premature urgency, and identity projection.

Synthetic benchmark reports must be labeled `development-only-not-empirical`. Statistical superiority may not be claimed from development fixtures.
