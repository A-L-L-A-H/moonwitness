# RGBL v4.0 — Domain Epistemology

The four RGBL levels are universal; **source roles are domain-sensitive**.

## Nature
Observation/measurement are primary for natural mechanisms. Philosophical meaning must not be presented as measurement.

## Human
Behavioral observation and first-person testimony have different roles. External behavior alone does not prove internal intention.

## Justice
Fact-finding, documentary/legal rules, testimony, impacts, and normative proportionality must remain distinguishable. A rule citation does not establish what factually occurred.

## Allah / Theology
- observable creation may provide empirical context/signs;
- philosophical reasoning may organize ultimate-source questions;
- revelation may support theological claims within the declared theological tradition;
- RGBL does **not** treat Allah as an empirically measurable object.

The engine exposes default admissibility profiles as quality guards, not as substitutes for domain scholarship.
