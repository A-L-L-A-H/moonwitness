# RGBL v7.0 — Research Governance

## Change classes
- **Editorial:** wording/examples only; no behavior change.
- **Additive research tooling:** new metrics/schemas without changing canonical RGBL yields.
- **Method change:** alters admissibility, transition, confidence, or diagnostic semantics; requires documented rationale and regression tests.
- **Canonical change:** attempts to rename/add/remove RGBL levels or yields; requires a new major research decision and cannot be silently introduced.

## Claim discipline
Repository documentation must label evidence status. `implemented`, `tested on synthetic fixtures`, `empirically evaluated`, and `externally validated` are separate statements.

## Theological discipline
CRAYON-ALLAH keeps empirical signs, philosophical reasoning, and revelation-based theological claims distinct. Framework implementation is a human research artifact; it is not presented as revelation or as Allah's literal speech.

## Release discipline
v7 is a stable research-engineering baseline. Future empirical evidence may strengthen, revise, or falsify assumptions without rewriting past results.
