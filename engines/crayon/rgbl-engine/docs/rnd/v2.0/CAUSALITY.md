# RGBL v2.0 — Hypothesis, Causality & Counterfactual Discipline

G2 must distinguish **pattern/association** from **causal explanation**.

## Causal checklist
A causal claim records, where relevant:
- temporal order;
- plausible mechanism;
- confounding addressed;
- competing explanations compared;
- intervention or other discriminating evidence.

A score is a workflow summary, not proof. Domain-specific standards can be stronger.

## Multiple hypotheses
A preferred hypothesis must remain alongside supporting evidence, opposing evidence, and evidence that could discriminate between alternatives. One narrative with no stated discriminating test triggers a lock-in warning.

## Counterfactuals
Counterfactual claims must expose assumptions and confidence. `If X had not occurred, Y would not have happened` is not treated as an observation.
