# RGBL v1.0 — Reliability & Agreement

RGBL should be classifiable by more than one person without requiring the framework author to explain every case.

## Required reporting
- number of compared labels;
- raw agreement;
- Cohen's kappa for pairwise categorical RGBL labels when applicable;
- R1/G2/B3/L4 confusion matrix;
- per-level precision, recall, F1, and support;
- repeated-run stability for the same analyst or system;
- preserved disagreement records before adjudication.

No single threshold is declared universal. Domain, case complexity, prevalence, sample size, and study design must be reported alongside metrics.

## Adjudication
Original labels remain immutable. A third-stage adjudication record may explain the adopted interpretation, but disagreement is research data—not noise to erase.
