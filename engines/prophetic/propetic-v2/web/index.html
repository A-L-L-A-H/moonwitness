Source Model

REVEALED SOURCE ≠ EXTANT TEXTUAL WITNESS

Qur’an is the project core/muhaimin source. Taurat, Zabur, and Injil are supplementary categories mapped to labeled textual witnesses. Extant witness corpora never silently become the original revelation.

TAURAT
Torah/Pentateuch witness; not automatically the entire Old Testament.
ZABUR
Psalms witness with attribution metadata; not every Psalm automatically equated with original Zabur.
INJIL
Gospel witness layer; not automatically the entire New Testament or identical to a reconstructed original Injil.
