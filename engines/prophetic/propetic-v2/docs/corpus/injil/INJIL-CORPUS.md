# Injil Corpus Policy

## Role

`INJIL` is a supplementary source category. The current working witnesses are canonical Gospel texts used as extant textual witnesses.

## Critical distinction

The engine must never silently encode:

`INJIL = entire New Testament`

or

`INJIL = one extant Gospel manuscript with perfect identity to the original revelation`.

Instead:

`INJIL source category → Gospel textual witness → Gospel/chapter/verse`.

## Language metadata

The language of an extant textual witness is separate from the language spoken by Isa and separate again from the language of the original revelation. Greek Gospel transmission, Aramaic/Hebrew historical milieu, and Qur'anic Arabic name forms must not be merged into one field.

## Required metadata

- Gospel witness name;
- chapter/verse;
- edition/translation when quoted;
- identity binding confidence;
- cross-source relation;
- language/transmission notes where material.
