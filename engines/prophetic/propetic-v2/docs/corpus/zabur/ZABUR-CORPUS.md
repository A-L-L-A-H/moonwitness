# Zabur Corpus Policy

## Role

`ZABUR` is a supplementary source category. The current working witness is the Psalms corpus.

## Attribution rule

Psalm attribution must remain metadata, not an automatic historical fact. A heading or traditional attribution to David may support a witness relation, but confidence and textual context must remain visible.

## Required metadata

- psalm number and verse;
- textual witness/edition when quoted;
- attribution status;
- prophet-binding confidence;
- claim relation to Qur'anic evidence.

## Guardrail

The model does not silently assert that every Psalm is the original Zabur revelation to Dawud. `ZABUR` and `Psalms textual witness` remain distinct nodes.
