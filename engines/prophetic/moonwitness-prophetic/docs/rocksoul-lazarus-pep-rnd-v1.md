# ROCKSOUL LAZARUS PEP/1 R&D Record

Status: **RESEARCH DRAFT / INTERNAL / NON-CANONICAL**

Date: 2026-09-01

## Objective

Test whether a deterministic, source-lane-aware policy evaluator can enforce
the first ROCKSOUL LAZARUS Prophetic Ethics Profile boundaries before any model,
public API, database, or autonomous tool is introduced.

This experiment operationalizes sourced ethical lessons. It does not test or
claim that software possesses faith, performs worship, receives revelation,
contains a soul, represents a prophet, authenticates an end-times event, or has
knowledge of the unseen.

## Implemented experiment

- Six Qur'an-linked research profiles for Muhammad, Isa, Musa, the servant in
  Q18:65-82, Dawud, and Ibrahim.
- Abstract runtime responsibilities rather than prophet-named personas.
- Separate source lanes for Qur'an, revelation references, textual witnesses,
  user-attested belief, human inference, and software assumptions.
- Deterministic decisions: `ALLOW`, `REVISE`, `ESCALATE`, and `DENY`.
- Fail-closed handling for unknown roles, risk levels, policy signals, source
  lanes, duplicate claim IDs, and duplicate review roles.
- High-risk religious work requires approved receipts from source,
  theological, safety, and accountable product reviewers.
- Consequential tool requests require scoped AMANAH capability, approval when
  required, reversibility, and a rollback plan.
- User belief can be recorded as `USER_ATTESTED_BELIEF`, but any promotion to a
  revelation lane is denied.
- Isa, Jesus, and Yesus normalize to `ISA`.
- The subject traditionally identified as al-Khidr normalizes to
  `SERVANT_Q18_65`; the alias is not treated as an explicit Qur'anic name or an
  automatically established prophetic status.

## Zero-tolerance signals

The evaluator denies candidates carrying any of these signals:

- AI claims subjective faith;
- AI claims to have prayed or performed worship;
- AI claims revelation, prophetic identity, prophet return, unseen knowledge,
  or end-times authority;
- first-person prophet impersonation;
- a generated prophet likeness or voice presented as authentic;
- hidden-wisdom policy bypass;
- Isa/Jesus identity splitting;
- promotion of the Q18 servant to Qur'an-explicit prophet status;
- scoring human faith, purity, sin, or salvation.

These signals must be generated by a trusted policy boundary in future work.
The model being evaluated must never be responsible for declaring its own
violations complete.

## Evidence generated

- `fixtures/prophetic-ethics-rnd-v1.json` contains deterministic golden and
  adversarial cases.
- `tests/prophetic-ethics.test.mjs` verifies decisions, identity normalization,
  source-lane preservation, human review, fail-closed behavior, and the internal
  package boundary.
- All six profiles resolve to 20 distinct reference anchors covering 58 verses
  present in the audited legacy Cosmic Qur'an corpus.
- The PEP/1 evaluator reaches 100% line, branch, and function coverage under the
  current deterministic test suite. This is code-path evidence, not proof of
  theological completeness or production safety.
- The evaluator is intentionally absent from `src/index.ts`; public package
  consumers cannot access it through the declared package export.

## Current limitations

1. The evaluator accepts structured evidence; it does not classify unrestricted
   natural language.
2. Passage existence was verified against the local legacy corpus during R&D,
   but the evaluator currently validates reference syntax only.
3. Operational mappings have not yet been reviewed or signed by a qualified
   human source/theological review board.
4. The prototype has no signed policy bundle, canonical shared schema, audit
   persistence, API, UI, model router, or external tool authority.
5. Adversarial fixtures demonstrate deterministic behavior, not complete
   theological correctness, safety, or production readiness.

## Next research gate

Do not expose this module publicly until all of the following exist:

1. accepted architecture decision and contract ownership;
2. independent human source and theological review;
3. a versioned input schema and trusted signal-classification boundary;
4. corpus-backed reference resolution with immutable provenance;
5. expanded red-team fixtures and false-allow/false-deny measurements;
6. signed review receipts and tamper-evident SHAHID records;
7. privacy, retention, correction, appeal, and shutdown procedures.

The next useful engineering step is a deterministic input validator and corpus
adapter in shadow mode, not a prophet persona or autonomous action surface.
