# ISA/Jesus Pilot Research Run — v1.0

## Purpose

This pilot demonstrates the end-to-end research workflow on one subject: Isa/Jesus.
It intentionally separates Quranic revelation claims from Christian Gospel texts and from historical/manuscript questions.

## Primary findings

1. Quran 5:46 explicitly states that Allah gave Isa the Injil. This establishes the Quranic revelation claim but does not identify the Injil with Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John.
2. Quran 4:171 explicitly identifies the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, as a messenger of Allah and affirms one God.
3. Matthew 24:1-2 is a canonical Gospel passage attributing a temple-destruction prediction to Jesus. Its presence in Matthew establishes a textual witness within the Christian canon; it does not, by itself, establish that Matthew is the original Injil given to Isa.

## Evidence boundaries

- Revelation status: assessed from the source tradition itself.
- Textual status: assessed from surviving texts and manuscript history.
- Historical status: assessed from independent historical evidence.
- Identification status: asks whether a surviving text can be linked to the specific revelation named by the Quran.

## Pilot conclusion

The key unresolved link is:

ALLAH -> ISA -> INJIL -> ? -> surviving Gospel text

The project must not silently replace the '?' with Matthew/Mark/Luke/John without evidence.
