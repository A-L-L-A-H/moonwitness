# Phase 11 — Claim Database

A claim is a research unit. A claim must point to a source and must not silently combine text, interpretation and historical inference.

Fields: `claim_id`, `topic`, `claim`, `source_type`, `source_level`, `reference`, `status`, `score`.

Important distinctions:
- `QURAN_EXPLICIT` means the Quran explicitly makes the claim.
- `TO_BE_VERIFIED` means an identification or historical proposition requiring evidence.
