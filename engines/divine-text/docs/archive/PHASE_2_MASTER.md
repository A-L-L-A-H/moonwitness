# Phase 2 Master — Muhammad ﷺ

## A. Quran

- Source type: `QURAN`
- Primary status in project: `L6 REVELATION_EXPLICIT`
- Audit focus: early manuscripts, rasm, readings/qira'at, variants, modern editions, provenance limits.
- Evidence card: `QURAN-TX-001`

## B. Hadith

- Source type: `HADITH`
- Primary status in project: `L4 PROPHETIC_REPORT`
- Audit focus: isnad, narrators, continuity, contemporaneity, matn, parallel routes, wording variants, classical grading, historical assessment.
- Evidence cards: `HADITH-001`, `HADITH-002`, `HADITH-METHODOLOGY-001`

## C. Sunnah

Treat as an interpretive umbrella for prophetic practice reconstructed from multiple reports. Do not model it as one manuscript.

## D. Sirah

Historical/biographical evidence. Keep it separate from revelation and from individual hadith grading.

## E. Tafsir

Interpretation layer. Preserve the underlying Quran citation and the commentator's claim separately.

## Phase 2 rule

No single source category inherits the authority, certainty, or score of another category.
